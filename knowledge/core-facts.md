# Core facts (maintainer-verified; use these exactly)
- Version names: after 1.21.11 (Dec 2025) Minecraft switched to year-based names such as 26.1, 26.2, 26.3. They have NO leading "1." - never write "1.26.3". Always get the newest ones from the minecraft_versions tool.
- Unbreaking: each use, the item takes durability damage with chance 1/(level+1) for tools and weapons. That is 50% (I), 33% (II), 25% (III) of uses, so the item lasts about 2x, 3x, 4x longer. Armor is different: it ignores damage 20% / 27% / 30% of the time (I / II / III).
- Anvil: 3 blocks of iron on the top row, 1 iron ingot in the middle of the middle row, 3 iron ingots on the bottom row.
- Netherite ingot: craft 4 netherite scrap + 4 gold ingots (shapeless) in a crafting table; 9 ingots come from 1 block of netherite. Upgrading gear needs a smithing table + netherite upgrade template.
- Golden apple: 8 gold ingots around 1 apple (full 3x3 ring). The enchanted golden apple is NOT craftable.
